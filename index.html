    Saved on this device

    Changes save on this device as you go. With sync turned on, they also go to your private notes repository so your other devices can pick them up.

    Edit everything

    The whole document in Markdown. Each line starting with ## begins a section. To-dos are lines like - [ ] Buy milk; - [x] marks one done. A line like <!-- keep: group: Money; short: Money; blurb: …; private --> under a section title sets its group, short name, summary and privacy.

    Sync settings

    Your notes are kept in one file in a private GitHub repository. Every device you connect reads and writes that file, so changes on your phone show up on your computer and the other way round.
